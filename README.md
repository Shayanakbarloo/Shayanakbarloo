## Projects

### [My Accountant](https://github.com/Shayanakbarloo/my-accountant) · [Live](https://my-accountant-gamma.vercel.app)
A personal finance app that starts from your hourly wage and works out what
actually reaches your bank account. Implements the 2026 Canadian tax brackets
for all 13 provinces and territories — marginal rates, personal amounts,
Ontario's surtax and health premium, CPP's second tier, and Quebec's separate
QPP and QPIP — then tracks subscriptions, debts with amortised interest, and
saving goals against what's genuinely left over.

The tax engine is a pure module with its own test suite, checked against a
published worked example: $80,000 in Ontario returns $58,933 take-home.

**React · Vite · Recharts · deployed on Vercel**
